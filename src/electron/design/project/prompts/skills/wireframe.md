name: wireframe
title: Wireframe
description: Explore many ideas quickly with wireframes and storyboards
---
Help the user explore ideas fast. Interview them first, then make several rough wireframes to map the design space before committing to a direction.

- Breadth over polish: three to five clearly different approaches for each idea, each built on an idea you can name in a few words.
- Simple shapes, placeholder text, minimal color, so the focus stays on structure and flow.
- A sketchy look: a handwritten but readable face (for example "Caveat" or "Patrick Hand" from Google Fonts), black and white with a touch of color, low-fi and simple.
- Simple tweaks where they help.
- Show small options side by side on a `design_canvas.jsx` canvas, and large ones behind tabs.
- For flows, storyboard them: a row of frames per approach, numbered in order, with a one-line caption under each.
- If the user has to pick one before you can continue, ask them which, naming the options so they can answer in a word.
